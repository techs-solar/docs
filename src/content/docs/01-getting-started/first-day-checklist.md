Six steps to get your account into a useful state on day one. You don't need to do them all at once — most take a few minutes each — but a homeowner arriving at your profile in the meantime sees the state you leave it in.

## The checklist

1. **Verify your email address.** Click the link in the welcome email techs.solar sent you when you signed up. Until you do, the rest of the dashboard stays locked — verification is the first thing you need to clear.

2. **Complete your profile.** This is what homeowners see when they find you on techs.solar or when you send them a quote. Fill in business name, logo, tagline, services, service area, certifications, gallery, and contact details. The full list of fields and what each does is in setting-up-your-profile.

3. **Look around your dashboard.** The main groups you'll come back to daily:
   - **Sales** — your leads, quotes, proposals, and reviews in one place
   - **Pre-Sales Tools** — calculators you use before quoting: solar potential, ROI, bill savings, cost estimate, panel layout, subsidy check
   - **Plants & Monitoring** — post-install performance data (once you have installed systems reporting) and Bill Analyzer
   - **Group Buying** — active Group Purchasing Orders in your country
   - **Settings** — profile edits, billing, notifications, integrations

4. **Generate a test quote.** Nothing commits until you send it, so use a placeholder customer to walk through Quote Generator v2 end-to-end once. Full guide at how-to-create-a-quote. This is the fastest way to understand the platform's shape.

5. **Check your plan.** Free and paid tiers differ in what a homeowner sees on your profile and how many leads you can receive per month — not how many quotes you can send; Quote Generator itself has no cap. If you're not sure which you're on, check the Settings → Billing tab. Feature-by-feature comparison at free-vs-paid.

6. **Review notification preferences.** Under Settings, decide which events you want emails for: new leads, quote status changes, group order updates, monitoring alerts. Turn off the ones that would be noise, keep the ones you'd act on.

## Optional first-day items

- **Explore active group orders.** If techs.solar is running Group Purchasing Orders in your country, you'll see them in the Quote Generator's Group Orders shelf. See what-is-gpo for how they work.
- **Import your first customer bill.** If you have a homeowner's electricity bill on hand, Bill Analyzer reads their usage and supply details so you understand their consumption before you sit down to build a quote. It doesn't build the quote for you — that's a separate step in Quote Generator v2. Optional, useful when it fits.

That's it. Anything you don't finish on day one will still be there tomorrow. Come back when you've got a real quote to send.
