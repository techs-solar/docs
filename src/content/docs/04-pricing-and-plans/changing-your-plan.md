This page walks through how to move between plans on techs.solar — upgrading from Free to paid, changing card details, cancelling an active subscription, and what to do inside the pre-capture cancellation window. Feature-by-feature plan comparison lives in free-vs-paid and [Founding Member vs Standard plan — what's the difference?](/04-pricing-and-plans/founding-member-vs-standard/); this page is about the mechanics of switching.

## The three tiers today

- **Free** — the default state after registration. Limited profile visibility, capped quote count, restricted feature access.
- **Early Bird 2026** — the introductory paid tier for accounts that joined during the launch window. Same feature set as Standard, at a lower rate that persists year over year for as long as you keep the subscription active.
- **Standard** — the regular paid tier for accounts that join after the Early Bird window closes.

You're on exactly one tier at a time. The tier is shown at the top of your Billing page.

## Upgrading from Free to paid

Open **Settings → Billing** and pick the checkout flow shown there. techs.solar selects a payment processor for you (Stripe or Viva) to complete the subscription — which one depends on your account and product, not something you choose yourself. When checkout completes, your tier updates automatically — no separate action needed on your end.

The Founding Member offer, where it's open, has its own checkout accessible from a dedicated page. Availability is per-country and limited by remaining slots in your local area — it can close before a set date if slots run out. See [Founding Member vs Standard plan — what's the difference?](/04-pricing-and-plans/founding-member-vs-standard/) for what it costs and what it includes beyond a standard subscription.

## Changing your card, updating billing details, or cancelling an active subscription

Once you have an active paid subscription, your Billing page has an **Open Billing Portal** button that hands you off to your payment processor's own customer portal, where you can update your card and manage your subscription. This currently works reliably for Stripe subscriptions; if you're on Viva and the portal doesn't open, use **Contact support** instead — Viva's self-service portal isn't live yet.

Cancelling from the portal is meant to take effect at the end of your current billing period, not immediately — but always check the confirmation screen the portal shows you before you close it, since the exact behavior is controlled by the payment processor.

## The 24-hour cancellation window

If you've just authorized a payment but it hasn't been captured yet (this applies mainly to Founding Member and similar delayed-capture flows), you have a **24-hour self-service cancellation window** directly from your Billing page. After 24 hours pass, or once the payment is captured, that self-service button disappears and cancellation moves through the processor portal above.

## Refunds

Refunds aren't self-service. Within 14 days of your payment being captured, your Billing page shows a money-back guarantee notice with a **Contact support** link that emails `support@techs.solar` directly. Outside that window, refunds are handled case by case through the same support channel. See refunds-policy for the specifics.
