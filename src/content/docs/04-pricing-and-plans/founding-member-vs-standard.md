Both plans give you full platform access. The difference is price, timing, and two permanent benefits that only Founding Members get.

## Side by side

| | Founding Member | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Annual price | €149 (locked 3 years) | €298/yr (or current rate) |
| When available | Limited — early launch period only | Always |
| Verified badge | ✅ | ✅ |
| Unlimited leads | ✅ | ✅ |
| GPO access | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Founding Member badge** | ✅ permanent | ❌ |
| **One local area free for life** | ✅ | ❌ |

## What "Founding Member badge" means

The badge appears on your profile permanently — it signals to homeowners that you were among the first professionals to commit to the platform in your country. It never expires, even if you later move to the Standard plan.

## What "one local area free for life" means

Paid-tier installers can pay extra to claim priority placement in specific local areas (municipalities). Founding Members get their home local area included in their subscription at no extra cost — forever. This is separate from the annual subscription price.

## The honest summary

If you're joining during the launch period, Founding Member is strictly better than Standard: same features, half the price for 3 years, plus two permanent benefits. The only reason to choose Standard is if the Founding Member cohort for your country is already full.
