This page describes how refunds work on techs.solar in plain terms. The binding version lives in the Terms of Service you agreed to at checkout — this page helps you understand it and know when to ask. For jurisdiction-specific advice (consumer distance-selling rules, B2B contract law), consult a lawyer — this describes platform behavior, not what a court will decide.

## The 14-day money-back guarantee

Every new paid subscription comes with a **14-day money-back guarantee**. From the moment your first payment is captured, you have 14 days during which you can request a full refund with no reason required. During that window your Billing page shows a visible reminder banner with the exact expiry date and a **Contact support** link — you don't have to hunt for it.

The 14 days count from **payment capture**, not from checkout or authorization. For Founding Member and other delayed-capture flows, that means the clock starts when the payment actually moves — which can be days or weeks after you signed up.

## After the 14 days

Once the window closes, refunds move to **case-by-case**. The same **Contact support** channel handles them (`support@techs.solar`), and each request is looked at on its own facts — there's no fixed published list of what qualifies. If you're not sure whether your situation counts, ask; support will tell you where your request lands rather than you having to guess in advance.

## What refunds cover — and what they don't

- **In scope:** subscription charges (monthly, annual, Founding Member fees), Territory slot fees, other charges made by techs.solar directly to your card.
- **Out of scope:** anything you paid a third party for (a group order commitment, an installer certification body's fee, an energy supplier), and any consequential losses of any kind.

For **group order (GPO)** commitments specifically, the money you commit isn't a subscription charge and isn't handled by this policy at all. It's held in a public escrow program (a smart contract), not in Techs.Solar's account, and released to the supplier only in stages against signed milestone evidence — never forwarded all at once. Refunds there are automatic and enforced by code: if funding doesn't complete or a deadline lapses, your unreleased money comes back to you by itself. The signed Member Agreement is the binding source for how those funds are held and returned. See [GPO and smart-contract escrow — where your money sits and how it moves](/03-group-buying/gpo-and-smart-contracts/) for the mechanics and gpo-member-agreement-overview for the terms.

## How to request a refund

1. Open your Billing page (Settings → Billing)
2. Click **Contact support** (or email `support@techs.solar` directly)
3. Include your account email, the charge date, and a brief reason
4. Support reviews the request and gets back to you with a decision

Once approved, refunds are processed via your original payment method within **5 business days**.

## The pre-capture cancellation window (different mechanism)

If your payment has been authorized but not yet captured — this applies mainly to Founding Member and similar delayed-capture flows — you can cancel outright within 24 hours from the Billing page. That's not a refund, it's a cancellation of the pending authorization, so no money moves at all. See changing-your-plan for the mechanics.
