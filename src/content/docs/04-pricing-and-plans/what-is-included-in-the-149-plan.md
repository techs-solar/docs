The €149 Founding Member plan is a limited early-launch offer. It gives you full access to the platform for 2026, locked at this price for 3 years.

## What's included

| Feature | Founding Member (€149/yr) | Free (€0) |
|---|---|---|
| Leads per month | Unlimited | 3 |
| Verified badge on profile | ✅ | ❌ |
| Phone number shown on profile | ✅ | ❌ |
| Service area map on profile | ✅ | ❌ |
| Priority in search results | ✅ | ❌ |
| Gallery photos | 12 | 4 |
| Reserved subdomain | ✅ | ❌ |
| Group buying (GPO) access | ✅ | ❌ |
| Founding Member badge (permanent) | ✅ | ❌ |
| One local area included free for life | ✅ | ❌ |

## The price lock

€149/year is locked for 3 years (2026, 2027, 2028). After year 3 you move to the Standard plan rate (currently planned at €298/year) — but your Founding Member badge stays forever, and your included local area stays free for life.

## How payment works

You save your card at registration. No charge is made immediately — techs.solar authorises the card and captures the payment when the platform launches in your country. If the launch doesn't happen, the authorisation expires and nothing is charged.

## Who can get this price

The Founding Member price is only available during the early launch period. Once the cohort for your country fills, new installers join at the Standard rate.
