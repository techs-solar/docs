Every lead that lands in your dashboard sits at a specific stage in your sales pipeline. Moving leads through the stages as you work them keeps the Leads list useful — a quick glance tells you where each conversation is and what your next action should be.

## The six stages

techs.solar uses a fixed pipeline shape. Every lead is in exactly one of these states:

| Stage | What it means | Your typical next step |
|---|---|---|
| **New Lead** | Just arrived, no action taken yet | Contact the homeowner within your first-response window |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out (call, email, WhatsApp) but no visit booked yet | Follow up or book a survey visit |
| **Visit Booked** | An on-site survey or measurement is scheduled | Complete the visit, then generate a quote |
| **Quote Sent** | You've generated and sent a quote from Quote Generator | Follow up on the quote; wait for customer decision |
| **Won** | Customer accepted; the job is yours | Move to installation planning; monitoring setup after install |
| **Lost** | Customer went elsewhere, didn't respond, or the fit wasn't right | Move on; see handling-a-lost-lead for how to close cleanly |

The stages are visually colour-coded in the Leads list so you can scan by state without reading names.

## Where you change the stage

Switch to the **Pipeline** tab in your Leads section for the Kanban board view. Each lead is a card in its current stage's column; move it forward (or back) with the stage buttons on the card. Won and Lost aren't locked once set — you can move a lead back out of either if you marked it by mistake.

The lead's own detail page shows its stage as a read-only pill, plus **Mark Won** / **Mark Lost** actions once you're ready to close it out. Changes made from the Pipeline tab don't refresh automatically on the separate list view — revisit or reload to see the latest stage there.

## Why stages matter

- **Sorting** — you can filter your Leads list to just New or just Contacted to focus your next block of work
- **Handover clarity** — if you share your account with a partner, the pipeline stage tells them exactly where a lead is without a status meeting

## What the pipeline does NOT do

- It doesn't score leads. There's no "hot" or "cold" ranking today — that judgment stays with you.
- It doesn't auto-progress. You move a lead forward when you've done the work; the platform doesn't guess.
- It doesn't notify the homeowner of stage changes — the pipeline is your internal view.
