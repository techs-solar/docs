The Cost Estimator is the tool for the first *"how much will this cost me?"* conversation. Enter a system size, pick a component tier, and get an all-parts-and-labour ballpark broken down by line item — useful before you've priced specific brands, before you know the roof, and often before the customer is ready for a formal quote.

Reach for it when the customer wants a number for a rough size and you don't want to open the Quote Generator yet — fast, editable, a defensible starting figure by cost category. Once the conversation moves toward committing, switch to the Quote Generator for a customer-ready price.

## How to run it

1. From your sidebar, open **Pre-Sales Tools → Cost Estimator**.
2. Enter a **System size (kWp)** — defaults to 5.
3. Pick a **Component tier**: **Budget** (entry-level, tighter margins), **Standard** (mid-range, the default), or **Premium** (top-tier components).
4. Results update as you type.

## What the result tells you

A line-item table breaks the total down by component — Panels, Inverter, Mounting, DC/AC Switchboard, Cabling, Labour — followed by **Total (excl. VAT)**, a **+ VAT @ 6%** line, and **Total (incl. VAT)**. The tool always applies the Greek reduced VAT rate of 6% regardless of your account's country — it doesn't currently adjust for other jurisdictions.

## The real numbers

Per-kWp rates the tool uses today, in € excl. VAT:

| Component | Budget | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Panels | 250 | 350 | 500 |
| Inverter | 80 | 120 | 180 |
| Mounting | 50 | 80 | 120 |
| DC/AC Switchboard | 30 | 60 | 100 |
| Cabling | 30 | 40 | 60 |
| Labour | 100 | 150 | 200 |
| **Per-kWp total** | **€540** | **€800** | **€1,160** |

Switchboard is really a fixed cost — the per-kWp figure is a smoothed approximation, good for 3–15 kWp systems and rougher outside that range.

## Editing the rates for your business

If your actual supplier prices differ, use the **Adjust component rates — edit to refine** expander below the results. Edits apply for the current session only — nothing is saved, and reopening the tool returns to the defaults. Deliberate: this is a scratch pad, not a place for persistent rates.

## Difference from the ROI Calculator's install cost

Cost Estimator prices components + labour per tier, no permits or grid-connection fees. roi-calculator uses a single all-in figure (€1,300/kWp) that folds those in — so a 5 kWp system shows €4,000 excl. VAT here (Standard) but ~€6,500 all-in there. Both are correct for their purpose: one prices what's in the crate, the other the total a homeowner pays.

## The important caveat

**This is a ballpark, not a quote.** Use the Quote Generator for a real price with real brand names, quantities, and correctly-applied VAT. If you're outside Greece, confirm the right VAT treatment with your accountant — this tool's 6% won't reflect your local rate.
