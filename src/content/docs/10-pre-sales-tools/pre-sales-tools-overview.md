Pre-Sales Tools are six calculators for the conversations that happen **before** you send a customer a real quote — before they've committed, before you've measured anything, before you know what brands you'll install.

## Why they exist

A first conversation usually starts with a specific question: *will my roof work?* *how long until it pays back?* *what'll my bill look like?* *how much does this cost?* *how many panels fit?* *is there any government help?*

Each question has its own tool, usable in under a minute with whatever the customer already has to hand — no Quote Generator, no ordered equipment, no site visit required.

## The six tools

| Customer's question | Tool | What it takes | What it returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Will my roof work? | solar-potential | Address (any format worldwide) | Usable area, annual generation, monthly shape, suggested kWp |
| How long to pay back? | roi-calculator | Monthly bill + roof orientation + postcode | Payback years, annual savings, 20-year total, recommended kWp |
| What'll the bill look like? | bill-savings | Monthly bill + kWp + live tariff + export credit | Savings/month, savings/year, kWh generated, self-sufficiency % |
| How much does the install cost? | cost-estimator | kWp + component tier | Line-item cost table by category, total incl. Greek 6% VAT |
| How do panels fit on the roof? | panel-layout | Panel size + rows per section | Panel count, kWp, m² covered, generation estimate |
| What incentives apply? | subsidy-checker | (nothing — country-scoped, currently Greek only) | Current programme catalogue with status, amounts, eligibility |

## When to use which

Pre-sales conversations rarely follow one tool in isolation. A useful order:

1. **solar-potential** first — an address gets you a same-conversation feel for whether the roof is worth pursuing.
2. **bill-savings** or **roi-calculator** next — Bill Savings for a bill-shaped question, ROI Calculator for a payback-shaped one. Same underlying assumptions, consistent answers.
3. **cost-estimator** when price comes up — a defensible ballpark by component category before Quote Generator.
4. **panel-layout** when physical fit is the sticking point — how many panels, what orientation, where a chimney gets in the way.
5. **subsidy-checker** whenever incentives come up.

Any of them can start the conversation; none of them can end it.

## What Pre-Sales Tools are not

They're not:

- **A customer-ready quote.** For that, use Quote Generator v2 — see how-to-create-a-quote, which handles real brand names, quantities, VAT on actual line items, GPO offers, and a sendable PDF.
- **A site survey substitute.** The tools don't know pitch, shading over the day, structural constraints, or hidden roof damage — only a visit catches those.
- **A binding commitment.** The numbers are ballparks; every tool bakes in Greek defaults for irradiance, tariffs, and regulatory rates, and a real quote adjusts for the actual project.

Use them to get the customer to *yes, let's talk properly* — not to replace the properly-talking that comes next.
